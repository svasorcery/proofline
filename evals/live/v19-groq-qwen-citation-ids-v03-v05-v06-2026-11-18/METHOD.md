# V03, V05 and V06 with citation IDs

Three synthetic full-input cases, one POST for each in the stated order.
Groq Free, Qwen 3.8 27B medium, budget $0 and no retries. The runner
waits at least 65 seconds between sends and checks the last received
quota headers before the next send.

The new Compose database is separate from the demo and older trials.
Preflight matches each input against the corresponding full replay
snapshot and the model options and system instruction against v12.
The original output, resolved card, request and quota receipt are
stored separately. The app did not publish a port.

The read-only v3 reference evaluator is used offline on the new
cards. Its synthetic references have no independent human validation.
The review distinguishes accepted wire format, application blockers
and the separate fact, evidence, chronology and follow-up criteria.
