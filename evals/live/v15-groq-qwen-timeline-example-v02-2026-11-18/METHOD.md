# V02 with a timeline example in citation-ID format

One synthetic V02 under the author's standing approval. Groq Free,
Qwen 3.8 27B with medium reasoning, $0, one POST, no retries.

The temporary schema matches v13 apart from citation IDs bound to the
new database. A fictional timeline example uses the actual citation-ID
format to illustrate supplier cancellation, form sending and form receipt.
The app separately blocks approval when the timeline omits a reviewed
event supported by the current selected source version.

The v14 experiment tested a wire minimum of three entries. Groq rejected
its generated output during schema validation. This trial uses the v13 wire
shape and leaves the completeness decision to the app. Preflight verifies
that the synthetic case input and model settings match.
